## Campaign settings

- Special Ad Category: Housing. No age, gender or ZIP targeting, 15 mile minimum radius.
- NY campaign: target New York (state) or NYC borough pins; you cannot exclude NJ, so expect some spill.
- To stop early calls, schedule ads 9:00 to 19:00, which needs a lifetime budget.
